## References and Attribution

The optimization framework is due to Tom Goertzen and Geordie Williamson:

- T. Goertzen and G. Williamson, *Kazhdan–Lusztig Basis and Optimization*, arXiv:2604.18894 (2026).
- T. Goertzen, [canonical-basis-optimizer](https://github.com/TomGoertzen/canonical-basis-optimizer), GitHub repository.

The present repository extends their optimization implementation by incorporating an additional longest-element intertwining constraint. Other computations concern evacuation-adapted weak Bruhat graphs and exact base-change matrices for Specht modules.

The exact Young seminormal basis calculations also build on the work of Armon and Halverson.

See `THIRD_PARTY.md` for further attribution and third-party software information.
